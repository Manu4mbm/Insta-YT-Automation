Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: 24 October 2026

The "Reels Automation-IG" app only stores your Instagram-scoped ID, comment ID and comment text when you comment on a @{{IG_HANDLE}} post, plus a log that a reply was sent.

How to request deletion

  1. Send a direct message to @{{IG_HANDLE}} on Instagram with the text DELETE MY DATA.
  2. We will delete all records linked to your Instagram account within 7 days and confirm by reply.

You can also remove the app's access at any time from Instagram: Settings → Website permissions → Apps and websites.

Records are deleted automatically after 90 days even without a request.
