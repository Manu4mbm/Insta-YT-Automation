Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

This policy covers the "Reels Automation-IG" app operated by {{OWNER_NAME}} for the Instagram account @{{IG_HANDLE}}. The app publishes {{OWNER_NAME}}'s own reels and sends a one-time reply message to people who comment a specific keyword on those reels.

What we collect

  • When you comment on a @{{IG_HANDLE}} post: your Instagram-scoped user ID, the comment ID and comment text, as sent to us by Instagram.
  • A log that a reply message was sent to that comment (to make sure we never message you more than once).

We do not collect passwords, contact lists, payment card or bank details, or any data from people who have not commented on our posts.

How we use it

  • Only to check if your comment contains the keyword and, if it does, send you the single requested message (for example a link to a free guide) and reply to your comment.
  • We do not sell, rent or share your data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

Purchases (paid kits)

When you buy a kit on /buy/… we collect your email address and mobile number, the order amount and the UPI reference number of your payment. We use them only to confirm your payment, deliver the kit (download link by email) and help you if something goes wrong. Card, UPI and netbanking payments are processed by Razorpay under its own privacy policy (or, when shown, paid by direct UPI to {{OWNER_NAME}}'s bank account); we never see or store your card number, bank login or UPI PIN. To confirm payments, an automated script in {{OWNER_NAME}}'s own Gmail reads only the bank's credit-alert emails. Order records are kept as long as required for tax and accounting purposes.

Storage and retention

Data is stored in a secured database (Supabase) and processed on Vercel servers. Comment logs are kept for up to 90 days and then deleted.

YouTube (Google API Services)

The app also uses the YouTube Data API, authorised only by {{OWNER_NAME}} for their own YouTube channel, to upload and schedule their own videos, set thumbnails, post a comment on their own videos and read their channel/video statistics. The OAuth refresh token is stored encrypted-at-rest in our database and is never shared. We do not access, store or share any YouTube data of viewers. Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Access can be revoked at any time at Google Account permissions. YouTube is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.

Your rights / deletion

You can ask us to delete your data at any time. See Data Deletion Instructions.
