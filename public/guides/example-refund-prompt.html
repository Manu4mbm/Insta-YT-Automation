Refund atka hai? AI se complaint likhwao.

Ye prompt Claude ya ChatGPT mein daalo. Ye aapki problem ko ek formal complaint, helpline ke liye short text, aur zarurat pade to consumer court ka draft bana deta hai.

1Prompt copy karo

Act as an expert in Indian consumer rights. Help me get my refund by writing a formal complaint. My details: [Name, city, email/phone] Company: [company name + website/app] Order / booking / transaction: [order ID, date, amount, payment mode] What went wrong: [explain the problem in 2–3 lines] What I already tried: [dates of calls/emails/chats, ticket numbers, what they replied] What I want: [full refund / replacement / charge reversal + compensation if any] Please write: 1. A firm but polite complaint email to the company's grievance officer, with a subject line and a clear deadline of 15 days. 2. A short version of the same complaint that I can paste on the National Consumer Helpline (website, app or WhatsApp). 3. A simple timeline of events, date-wise. 4. A list of documents and screenshots I should attach. 5. If the company still doesn't resolve it: a draft complaint for the District Consumer Commission that I can file on e-Jagriti — facts of the case, deficiency in service / unfair trade practice under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019, and the relief I am asking for. Use simple language. Do not invent any facts — if something is missing, ask me first.

2Pehle company ko bhejo

AI wala email company ke grievance officer ko bhejo. Unka email aksar app/website ke "Grievance" ya "Contact us" page pe hota hai. Screenshot aur order details attach karo, aur ticket number save rakho.

3Na sune to National Consumer Helpline

📞 1915 / 1800-11-4000toll-free · 8 AM–8 PM
💬 WhatsApp+91 88000 01915
📱 NCH App / UMANGapp se complaint
🌐 Websiteconsumerhelpline.gov.in

Prompt ka short version (point 2) yahan paste karo. Complaint number zaroor note karo.

4Phir bhi na maane: consumer court

Point 5 wala draft lekar e-Jagriti portal pe District Consumer Commission mein online complaint file kar sakte ho. Filing ke rules aur fees portal pe check karo.

📎 Ye ready rakho

  • Order/booking ID, invoice, payment proof
  • Chat/email/call ke screenshots (date ke saath)
  • Ticket ya complaint numbers
  • Product ki photo/video (agar damaged hai)

⚠️ Ye guide general jaankari ke liye hai, legal advice nahi. Bade amount ya complicated case mein kisi lawyer se salah lo. AI ka draft bhejne se pehle ek baar khud padh ke check karo.
