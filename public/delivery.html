Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

All products sold on this website are digital downloads (kits, templates, guides). Nothing is shipped physically.

  • Delivery time: instantly after successful payment — a download button appears on the checkout page and the download link is emailed to you, usually within 1–5 minutes.
  • Delivery address: the email you enter at checkout. You can correct it on the checkout page if you made a typo.
  • Not received? Check Spam/Promotions, or contact us with your order number and we will send it manually within 24 hours.
