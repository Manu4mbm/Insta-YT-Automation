# Viral reel playbook (learned from a live AI-tools channel, Oct–Nov 2026)

These are patterns from real data (13 published reels, 7 competitor accounts). Treat them as strong hints, not laws — re-check with `/ig-audit` on your own account every few weeks.

## What separated winners from flops
| Signal | Winners | Flops |
|---|---|---|
| What the viewer gets | a FREE, useful resource for a pain they already feel | "look what I made" showcases / product promos |
| Shares ÷ reach | ~3–4% | under 0.7% |
| Saves ÷ reach | ~4.5–5.5% | under 2% |
| Avg watch | 14–24 s | 6–9 s (hook failed in the first 2 s) |

## Hook rules
- Line 1 = a pain with money/time + a promise ("X ka bill dekh ke…", "Refund nahi de rahi? Ye prompt copy karo").
- Mass topic beats niche topic: problems everyone has (refunds, bills, free alternatives) travel to non-followers.
- First frame must move and must show the payoff visually (the tool, the result, the number).
- Open loop: a small sticker like "🔒 full prompt at the end" keeps people to the CTA.

## Format rules
- One format that worked = repeat it. Don't switch styles every reel.
- 35–45 s, a visual change every 1–2 s, word-synced captions, SFX on every cut.
- 3 steps, plain words, no jargon. Verify every fact the week you post it, and show the source on screen.
- End: "Comment KEYWORD" + follow (the DM funnel's follow-gate turns commenters into followers) + "send this to the friend who…" + "save this".

## Posting
- Best slot in our tests: 13:30 IST (12:00–15:00 window). Early morning and evening were weak.
- 1–2 reels/day beat 3/day (back-to-back reels split reach).
- Reaching only old followers? Post as a **trial reel** (`trial_strategy = 'SS_PERFORMANCE'`): Instagram shows it to non-followers first and auto-shares it to followers if it performs.
- Share to your story right after posting; reply to every comment in the first hour.

## Competitor research (outlier method)
Rank other creators' reels by views ÷ that account's median views, never by raw views. 3x+ is a signal; 50x+ is a formula worth copying (copy the formula, never the video or script).
