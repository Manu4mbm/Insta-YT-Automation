# Safe area & export (1080×1920)

- Organic Reels: keep key text out of top ~220 px (header), bottom ~400 px (caption/username/audio), right ~120 px (like/comment/share).
- **Reels ads (Meta guidance): top 14% (≈270 px), bottom 35% (≈670 px), 6% each side (≈65 px) free of key text.** → key text in y 270–1250, x 65–1015.
- Decoration, textures, oversized glyphs may bleed into these zones.
- Export: 1080×1920, 30 fps, MP4 H.264 (`--crf=18`), AAC.
- Cover: 1080×1920 PNG, hook inside centre 3:4 (y ≈ 240–1680) for the profile-grid crop.
- Before posting: watch on phone sound on AND off; every hero word readable at first glance; no text flashing < 0.4 s; rights to audio.
