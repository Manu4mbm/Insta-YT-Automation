# /red-kinetic-collage-edit

**Input:** a 9:16 talking-head clip

## What it does
 Black-and-white footage with one glowing red word and collage-style type.

## Steps
1. Turn my footage black and white with strong contrast.
2. Add cut-out collage text and paper textures.
3. Make one key word per sentence glow red.
4. Add quick shake and zoom on emphasis words.

- Show me 3 snapshots before the final render, and ask before overwriting files.
