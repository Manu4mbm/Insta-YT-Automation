15 August 1947... Bharat azaad hua, par ek nahi tha.
Desh ke andar thi 565 riyasatein, raja aur nawab, jinhe Angrezon ke jaane ke baad chunna tha: India, Pakistan, ya azaadi.
Ye zimmedari mili Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel aur unke secretary V. P. Menon ko.
Ek kaagaz bana, Instrument of Accession. Sirf teen vishay: raksha, videsh aur sanchar.
15 August tak lagbhag saari riyasatein jud chuki thin... bas kuch baaki thin.
Junagadh: February 1948 ke janmat sangrah ke baad Bharat ka hissa bana.
Hyderabad: 13 September 1948 ko Operation Polo, aur 17 September ko Nizam ne haar maan li.
Jammu-Kashmir: Maharaja ne 26 October 1947 ko Instrument of Accession par sign kiya.
565 tukde... ek naksha. Isliye Patel kehlaye Bharat ke Loh Purush.
Aisi aur kahaniyon ke liye comment karo PATEL, aur follow karo India by Bharat.
